Privacy Policy

云晞心流 (Yohe Aura) · Effective date: September 9, 2026

Yohe Aura ("the App", "we", "our") is a card-based self-exploration and daily guidance application. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share your personal identity information. We do not require an account to use core features, and we do not sell any data to third parties.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your reading history, preferences, unlocked items, and app settings are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

3. Optional Login

If you choose to sign in (e.g., with Google), we use the sign-in service solely to identify you within the app. The sign-in provider may process data according to its own privacy policy.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (subscriptions and stardust) are processed by Google Play and our payment service provider. We do not see or store your full payment card details.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing — processes payments.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription entitlements.

These providers may receive limited technical information necessary to provide their services, subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
